This is an unofficial web edition of _Do Business Without Intellectual Property_, a guide by Stephan Kinsella first published as a PDF by Liberty.me in July 2014. Kinsella released the guide under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), which means anyone may copy, adapt and republish it. This site is not affiliated with Liberty.me.

## The text

Kinsella’s words are reproduced exactly as published. An automated check compares every word on this site against the original PDF, and the site cannot be published if a single word differs.

A few things did change, all of them typographic or caused by moving from print to the web:

- Six small slips in punctuation and spacing were fixed, such as a missing space and two mismatched quotation marks.
- Words split across lines in the PDF were rejoined, and headings set in capitals were written in title case.
- The original disclaimer named Liberty.me. It now refers to the editors of this edition instead.
- The original cover photo, author photo and Liberty.me branding are not reproduced.

Every change is listed in the [edition notes](https://github.com/vb6enjoyer/do-business-without-ip/blob/main/docs/EDITION.md).

## Links

The guide cites 41 sources. Many of them have moved or disappeared since 2014. Each footnote now records whether its link still works. When it doesn’t, or when the site can’t be checked automatically, the link goes to a copy saved by the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine, as close to July 2014 as possible. Links are rechecked every week.

## Since 2014

Some of the examples in the guide were news in 2014 and have moved on since. Short editorial updates, marked **Since 2014** in the text, explain what happened next. They are not part of Kinsella’s guide, and each one cites its sources.
