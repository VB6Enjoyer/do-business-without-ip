Intellectual property refers to laws that protect the products of the intellect, as opposed to laws dealing with ownership of commonplace physical or material goods. IP is a broad term that includes several types of legal rights: **copyright** (which gives authors a right in original works such as novels or paintings); **patent** (which gives inventors rights in practical inventions like a mousetrap); **trademark** (which gives companies rights in names used to identify products such as Coca-Cola); and **trade secret**. Trademark is said to have its basis in protecting consumers from deception and fraud by unscrupulous vendors who falsely use others’ names and reputations.
