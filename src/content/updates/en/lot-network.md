The kind of mechanism described here arrived the same month this guide was published. In July 2014 Asana, Canon, Dropbox, Google, Newegg and SAP launched the LOT (License on Transfer) Network. Members keep their patents, but if one is ever sold to a company that makes its money from patent lawsuits, every other member automatically gets a license to it. The network has since grown to thousands of member companies.
