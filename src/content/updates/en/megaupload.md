More than a decade after the 2012 raid on his home, New Zealand’s Justice Minister signed an order in August 2024 to surrender Kim Dotcom to the United States, where he faces charges including criminal copyright infringement and racketeering. Dotcom challenged the decision in court. In July 2026 New Zealand’s Court of Appeal rejected his latest challenge, leaving a possible appeal to the Supreme Court.
