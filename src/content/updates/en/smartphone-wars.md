Apple and Samsung dropped all their lawsuits outside the United States in 2014 and kept fighting only the original US case. They settled it in June 2018, seven years after Apple first sued, on undisclosed terms. By then Samsung had already paid Apple $399 million, and a jury had just awarded Apple another $539 million.
