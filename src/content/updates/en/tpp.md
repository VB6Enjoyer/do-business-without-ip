The United States withdrew from the Trans-Pacific Partnership in 2017. The other eleven countries went ahead with a revised pact, the CPTPP, and suspended most of the intellectual-property chapter the US had pushed for. That includes the rule requiring copyright to last for the author’s life plus 70 years. The provisions were suspended, not deleted: they can return if all members agree.
